Vita Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Vita is a private assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains what data Vita accesses and how it is handled.

What Vita accesses

With the owner's permission, Vita has access to the owner's own Google accounts:

  • Gmail: read access, to summarize email and identify messages awaiting a reply; and compose access, used only to save email drafts that the owner has asked for.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to list meetings and send reminders.
  • Google Tasks (read-only): to include tasks and deadlines in briefings.

Vita never sends email. Drafts stay in the owner's Drafts folder until the owner reviews and sends them. Vita does not delete, label, or change existing email, calendar events, or tasks.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce briefings, reminders, and answers that are sent to the owner by text message. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone other than the service providers below, which process it only to provide Vita's features:

  • Anthropic (Claude API), to write summaries and answers.
  • Photon, to deliver text messages to the owner.
  • Railway, which hosts the app.

Storage

Vita keeps its last 30 text messages with the owner so it can follow the conversation; these may include summaries of email or calendar details. Older messages are discarded automatically. Full email, calendar, and task contents are fetched when needed and are not kept. The owner can revoke Vita's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Vita's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
