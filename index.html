Vita

A private personal assistant

Vita is a personal executive assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign up for it.

Vita sends its owner text messages with a morning briefing, an afternoon check-in, and reminders before meetings, and answers the owner's questions about their own schedule and email. To do this, it reads (but does not change) the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks.
